Crêpes:
- [ ] 45g all-purpose flour (1/3 cup)
- [ ] 120ml whole milk (1/2 cup)
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp vanilla extract 
- [ ] 1 Egg
- [ ] 1 tbsp melted butter
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar
- [ ] Pinch of salt

Sauce:
- [ ] Juice of 1 orange
- [ ] Zest of 1 orange (optional)
- [ ] 65g sugar (1/3 cup)
- [ ] 3 Tbsp butter
- [ ] Splash Grand Manier
- [ ] Squeeze of Lime juice (optional)

Makes 4 crepes.

---

**Plating:** Serve with vanilla ice cream.
